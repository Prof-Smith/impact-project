Common Criteria

A shared university framework that programs interpret through discipline-specific standards.

01

Significance

Defines a meaningful problem, question, need, or opportunity.

02

Disciplinary foundation

Uses relevant knowledge, evidence, methods, theories, or professional standards.

03

Stakeholder awareness

Identifies the people, organizations, communities, or audiences affected by the work.

04

Responsibility

Addresses relevant ethical, privacy, safety, accessibility, security, and professional considerations.

05

Feasibility

Establishes an achievable scope within the available time, resources, access, and curricular setting.

06

Contribution

Produces a meaningful artifact, recommendation, intervention, design, investigation, or creative work.

07

Evaluation

Explains how quality, effectiveness, learning, or impact will be assessed.

08

Communication

Presents the work professionally to an appropriate audience.

09

Reflection

Examines limitations, tradeoffs, consequences, and learning.

Readiness is not sameness.

Every project should meet the common expectations, but the evidence, methods, artifact, and presentation should remain appropriate to the student's discipline and pathway.
