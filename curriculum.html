Four-Year Curriculum

A developmental pathway that builds clarity, capability, and ownership before the final project.

Year 1

Explore

What matters to me, and where might my education enable me to contribute?

Impact Exploration Statement

  • Academic and professional interests
  • Meaningful questions or opportunities
  • Connections to a major or career
  • Strengths and experiences to build upon
  • Questions for further exploration

Review: Completion and developmental guidance, not project approval.

Year 2

Investigate

What challenge or opportunity is worth investigating, and what does the evidence show?

Impact Inquiry Brief

  • Preliminary challenge or opportunity
  • Significance and affected stakeholders
  • Initial evidence
  • Disciplinary connection
  • Ethical or professional considerations
  • Possible project formats

Review: Developmental feedback. The topic remains provisional.

Year 3

Design

What will I create, investigate, improve, or contribute?

Impact Project Proposal

  • Challenge, significance, and stakeholders
  • Disciplinary foundation and evidence
  • Approach and intended contribution
  • Feasibility and responsibility
  • Milestones, evaluation, and communication

Review: Approved; approved with conditions; revise and resubmit; redirect; or specialized review required.

Year 4

Create and Demonstrate

How will I turn knowledge into a contribution I can explain, defend, and demonstrate?

Final Impact Project

  • Discipline-appropriate final artifact
  • Evidence of development and revision
  • Evaluation of results
  • Executive or public-facing summary
  • Reflection on learning and limitations
  • Presentation, demonstration, exhibition, publication, or defense

Review: Final program assessment.
