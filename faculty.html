Faculty Guidance

Common architecture supports institutional coherence; disciplinary ownership protects academic integrity.

Faculty role

  • Define discipline-specific expectations
  • Guide project development
  • Evaluate project feasibility
  • Address methods and professional standards
  • Determine whether specialized review is needed
  • Approve Year 3 proposals
  • Assess Year 4 completion

Program responsibilities

  • Map milestones to existing courses and advising
  • Identify reviewers
  • Approve appropriate pathways and artifacts
  • Maintain a discipline-specific rubric
  • Record completion
  • Accommodate transfer students and changes of major

Recommended implementation principle

Map first, add later.

Programs should identify where the four milestones already fit within the curriculum before proposing new courses or credits. This protects timely degree completion and avoids unnecessary curricular growth.

MilestonePotential curricular homeReview function
ExploreFirst-year seminar, introduction to major, advising, general educationCompletion and guidance
InvestigateGateway course, methods, writing, statistics, laboratory, or field courseDevelopmental feedback
DesignJunior seminar, pre-capstone, practicum preparationFormal approval
