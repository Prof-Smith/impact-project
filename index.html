The Impact Project

Knowledge Applied. Purpose Realized.

A four-year learning progression through which students explore an area of purpose, investigate a meaningful challenge, design a discipline-appropriate project, and create and communicate a substantial final contribution.

Explore the four-year journeyView project pathways
The institutional promise

One shared standard. Many authentic forms of impact.

Every student develops work that is meaningful, evidence-informed, responsible, feasible, and professionally communicated. Academic programs define what strong work looks like in their disciplines.

1

Explore

Identify interests, strengths, values, and areas of possible contribution.

2

Investigate

Examine a meaningful challenge or opportunity through evidence and disciplinary inquiry.

3

Design

Develop a focused, feasible, and responsible Impact Project proposal.

4

Create and Demonstrate

Complete the approved project and communicate its contribution to an appropriate audience.

Built for every discipline

Different projects. Common expectations.

The Impact Project is not a single standardized assignment. A management student may develop an organizational improvement plan. A computing student may produce a technical solution. An education student may design a learning intervention. A science student may complete an applied investigation. A creative student may produce public or cultural work.

Every Impact Project demonstrates

  • Significance and disciplinary grounding
  • Stakeholder awareness and responsibility
  • Feasibility and sustained development
  • A meaningful contribution
  • Evaluation, communication, and reflection
