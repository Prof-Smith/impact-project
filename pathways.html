Project Pathways

Seven broad pathways give students a clear point of entry while preserving disciplinary authenticity.

Pathways guide, rather than restrict. Programs determine which pathways and project forms are appropriate for their disciplines.
Pathway 1

Research and Inquiry

Scientific investigation, humanities scholarship, social inquiry, policy analysis, behavioral research, and program evaluation.

Illustrative projects

  • Applied research study
  • Policy analysis
  • Scientific investigation
  • Community needs assessment
  • Program evaluation
  • Historical or cultural inquiry
Pathway 2

Management and Organizational Impact

Organizational performance, leadership, operations, workforce development, project management, governance, and change.

Illustrative projects

  • Organizational improvement plan
  • Change-management initiative
  • Workforce-development program
  • Operations improvement
  • Project implementation plan
  • Family-business succession plan
Pathway 3

Applied Professional Practice

Discipline-based projects in finance, accounting, marketing, logistics, communication, education, criminal justice, social services, and related professions.

Illustrative projects

  • Financial analysis
  • Marketing strategy
  • Logistics improvement plan
  • Fraud-risk assessment
  • Communication campaign
  • Agency process improvement
Pathway 4

Design and Technology

Computing, cybersecurity, artificial intelligence, analytics, software, robotics, digital systems, and technology implementation.

Illustrative projects

  • Software prototype
  • Cybersecurity readiness plan
  • Data dashboard
  • AI application concept
  • Digital-transformation proposal
  • Technology implementation plan
Pathway 5

Enterprise and Innovation

New ventures, social enterprises, nonprofit initiatives, products, small-business growth, and internal innovation.

Illustrative projects

  • Venture proposal
  • Social-enterprise model
  • New product concept
  • Small-business growth plan
  • Nonprofit operating model
  • Organizational innovation initiative
Pathway 6

Education and Community Impact

Learning interventions, school initiatives, human-service programs, public service, prevention, and community partnerships.

Illustrative projects

  • Learning intervention
  • Financial-literacy initiative
  • School-improvement proposal
  • Community-service program
  • Family-support initiative
  • Public-safety or prevention project
Pathway 7

Creative and Public Impact

Creative writing, publications, exhibitions, media, cultural work, public communication, civic engagement, and community storytelling.

Illustrative projects

  • Creative publication
  • Exhibition
  • Documentary or media project
  • Public-information campaign
  • Oral-history project
  • Civic-engagement project
