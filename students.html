Student Guidance

Use the milestones and criteria to prepare strong work before entering your program's official review process.

How to use this site

  1. Review the milestone for your current academic year.
  2. Explore the approved project pathways.
  3. Identify the pathway that best fits your developing work.
  4. Review the common project criteria.
  5. Use the criteria to prepare or revise your annual milestone.
  6. Submit official work through the process designated by your instructor or academic program.

Before faculty evaluation

  • What challenge or opportunity am I addressing?
  • Why does it matter?
  • Who is affected?
  • What disciplinary knowledge will guide the work?
  • What will I create, investigate, improve, or contribute?
  • Is the project feasible?
  • What responsibilities apply?
  • How will I evaluate and communicate the work?
Official-process notice: This site provides guidance only. It does not accept submissions, provide evaluation, determine readiness, or indicate approval. Follow the process established by your academic program.
